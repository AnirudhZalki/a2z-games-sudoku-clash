Privacy Policy

Sudoku Clash · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Clash (“the App”) handles information when you play. The App is published by A2Z Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Please read this policy before using the App.

Information the App handles

  • Account information: If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password. If you use Google sign-in, Google provides account details needed for authentication, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and, when available, profile name. The App does not need or store your Google password.
  • Guest account identifiers: Online play and cloud features may create an anonymous Firebase account with an internal identifier, even if you do not register with an email address.
  • Game information: The App stores progress, streaks, match history, and performance information such as game mode, score, wins, mistakes, and play duration. Some of this information can be stored on your device and, when Firebase is available and you use connected features, synced to Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Online matches: To run a room match, the App processes a room code and player identifier, display name (or a generated player name), connection status, game progress, mistakes, completion status, and match result. This information is available to participants in that room.
  • Public leaderboard: If a score is published, your display name (or a generated player name) and score are visible to people using the leaderboard. Your email address is not displayed on the leaderboard.
  • App preferences: The App saves preferences such as your selected light or dark theme on your device.

How information is used

We use this information to:

  • provide sign-in, account recovery, and account syncing;
  • save game progress and show your personal game statistics;
  • create and operate online matches and leaderboards; and
  • remember your app preferences.

Where information is stored and who processes it

Information saved locally is stored by the App on your device. If you use account, leaderboard, or online-match features, information is sent to Google Firebase services configured for the App. Authentication may also use Google Sign-In when you choose that option. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms and policies. Online rooms are designed to share match information with their participants, and leaderboard names and scores are public to leaderboard users.

We do not sell personal information. The App currently does not include third-party advertising or an in-app analytics SDK. This may change in a future version; if it does, we will update this policy and our applicable Play Store disclosures.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Cloud information may remain in Firebase while your account or connected features are in use. To request deletion of your account and associated cloud information, contact us at the address below from the email associated with your account, if applicable. We will verify the request and delete data we control, subject to legal requirements and any limited retention needed to complete the request. Uninstalling the App alone does not delete cloud information.

Security

We use Firebase services and their security controls to support connected features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this same URL, with the updated date shown at the top.
